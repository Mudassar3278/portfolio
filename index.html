About Me

I am a software engineer with a BS in Computer Science from International Islamic University, Islamabad, and more than four years of professional experience building production web applications with PHP, Laravel and CodeIgniter.

My work spans subscription-based e-learning platforms, repair-shop websites with admin panels and quote widgets, a medical billing system and public business websites. In each of them I have focused on clean backend architecture, reliable database design and secure, well-documented APIs.

Why a Master’s degree? Production work showed me how much good engineering depends on strong foundations. I want to deepen my knowledge of software architecture, algorithms and data systems, and to work in a research-oriented, international environment where I can learn from experts and contribute my practical experience.
